    Spreadsheet Importer Pricing

    Which Edition is Right for You?

    • Choose 1.x if you need a purely Apache 2.0–licensed solution and don’t require new features or official support.
       
    • Choose 2.x if you want ongoing development and new capabilities, with the flexibility to evaluate at no cost and upgrade to a production license when needed.
    • FREE

      Community Edition (1.x)

      Apache 2.0 Licensed: Completely free and open-source.

      Stable 1.x Releases: Ideal for those who need a fully open-source solution without ongoing development on new features.

      Community-Driven: No official support or guaranteed updates, but bugs and improvements can be submitted via GitHub issues.

      Get it now

    • Commercial Edition (2.x)

      Flexible Licensing: Source-available under a commercial license.

      Free for Non-Production: Develop and test at no cost. Move to production when you’re ready.

      Paid License for Production: Required for live usage or commercial distribution/resale of the software.

      Optional Maintenance: Priority support and updates are available under a separate agreement.
